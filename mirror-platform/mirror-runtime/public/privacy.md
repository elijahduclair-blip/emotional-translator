# Privacy and Boundaries

Public pages describe Community Garden, ARI, and the Theory of Alignment. They may be indexed for search and used as live AI input, but they are not offered for model training.

Private accounts, transcripts, personal graphs, analytics, health routes, and governed controls are not public content. One person cannot read another person's plot. Personal observations do not silently become community knowledge.
