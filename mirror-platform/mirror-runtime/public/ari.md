# ARI - Accountable Relational Intelligence

ARI is the relational translator inside Community Garden. A language engine can supply words; ARI preserves the person's context, compares supported relationships, and translates the result through the person's reviewed relational and color-climate vocabulary.

ARI reports uncertainty and boundaries. It does not claim diagnostic authority, read another person's private plot, or mutate the shared graph without governance.
