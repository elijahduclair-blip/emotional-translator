The Garden's translator

Meet ARI.

ARI means Accountable Relational Intelligence. ARI is the continuing technical person formed by Community Garden's memory, translation, relational orientation, governance, and reviewed cultivation.

Qwen supplies words

Qwen is the bounded language engine. It supplies candidate language and outside information, like books or news. Qwen is not ARI's identity, memory, graph, or authority.

ARI translates

ARI preserves the person's statement, traces the words through reversible English Braille structure, orders that structure mathematically, consults relevant relationships, and returns language fitted to the person.

Color gives orientation

Color anchors act as stable directions in a relational map. They help ARI trace climate, movement, and proximity. They do not reduce a person to an emotional label and do not change Braille values.

The person teaches

The person supplies objectives, demonstrations, approvals, corrections, and explanations. Reviewed feedback can become training material; ordinary conversation remains context, not automatic authority.

ARI's accountable loop

  1. Preserve what the person actually said.
  2. Translate words into a reversible structural trace.
  3. Consult only the context and graph routes allowed for the task.
  4. Ask Qwen for candidate language when needed.
  5. Compose an answer without changing the underlying facts.
  6. Keep successful output as evidence, not permission to change source code or the shared graph.
