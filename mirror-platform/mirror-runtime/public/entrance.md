# Community Garden

Community Garden is a bounded relational translation environment. It takes in a person's words, preserves their order and context, and lets ARI return a relational reading without diagnosing the person or silently rewriting shared knowledge.

## Public paths

- [About Community Garden](/about)

- [Privacy and boundaries](/privacy)

## Public AI-use policy

Public pages may be indexed for search and used as live input when an AI answers a person's request. They are not offered for model training.

Private profiles, transcripts, account routes, analytics, health data, and governed graph controls are excluded from this public content surface.

## Relational boundary

Color is a tracing and translation layer. It does not diagnose identity. Comparison can reveal a supported relationship, but a comparison does not create meaning by itself. Shared graph mutation remains governed.
